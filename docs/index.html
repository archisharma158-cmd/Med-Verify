Capabilities & Innovations

Smart Screening Features

A multi-modal intelligence pipeline designed for accessibility, transparency, and consumer protection.

📱

QR & Barcode Scanning

Decodes 2D GS1 DataMatrix codes and standard retail barcodes to extract GTIN, batch numbers, and production identifiers instantly.

🟢 Implemented
📷

Packaging OCR Recognition

High-precision text extraction powered by OCR.space API reads expiry dates, manufacturing details, and drug brand names from camera photos.

🟢 Implemented
🏛️

CDSCO Regulatory Alert Match

Cross-references scanned medicines against imported CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation) Not-of-Standard-Quality safety notices.

🟢 Implemented
📊

Rule-Based Risk Scoring

Calculates a transparent 0–100 concern score categorized into Low (0-25), Medium (26-60), and High (61-100) risk levels with actionable advice.

🟢 Implemented
🗣️

Sarvam AI Hindi Voice Assistant

Integrates Sarvam AI Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech to deliver voice-based interactive search and audio results for rural accessibility.

🟢 Implemented
🤖

Google Gemini Explanations

Grounded LLM explanations convert technical pharmaceutical information into simple, clear bilingual summaries in English and Hindi.

🟢 Implemented
🚩

Suspicious Product Reporting

Enables users and healthcare workers to flag suspect packaging with photos, persisting reports to an administrative review dashboard.

🟢 Implemented
🔮

Computer-Vision Anomaly ML

Planned deep-learning vision model to identify packaging print anomalies, seal tampering, and counterfeit design variances.
